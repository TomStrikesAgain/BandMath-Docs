# Merch Stand & The Merch Warehouse

BandMath provides a complete, touring-grade merchandise management and Point-of-Sale ecosystem built around two connected hubs: the **Merch Warehouse** (where you manage your catalog, categories, and stock) and the **Merch Stand** (where you ring up fans at the show).

## 1. The Merch Warehouse (Inventory & Catalog Hub)

Accessible directly from the **Shop** tab, the **Merch Warehouse** is your centralized backroom for organizing products before and during a tour:

* **Custom Categories & Merch Types:** Organize your catalog into clean categories—T-Shirts, Hoodies, Vinyl, Cassettes, Hats, Posters, Stickers, or any custom merch type your band dreams up.
* **Size & Variant Matrix:** Track independent stock counts across every apparel size (`XS`, `S`, `M`, `L`, `XL`, `XXL`, `3XL`) as well as non-sized items (`N/A`).
* **Historical Batch Cost Tracking:** Because the cost of pressing vinyl or screen-printing shirts changes from run to run, BandMath records the exact unit production cost of every inventory batch you add. That way, your profit splits and [Sales Analyzer](./03_Sales_Analyzer.md) ROI metrics stay 100% historically accurate.
* **Low-Stock Visibility:** Spot depleted sizes before you load the van so you know exactly when to reorder with your printer.

## 2. The Merch Stand (Point-of-Sale System)

When the house lights come up and fifty fans rush the merch table at once, speed is everything:

* **Single-Tap Dynamic Cart:** Tap any item and size in the **Merch Stand** to drop it into your active **Cart**.
* **Atomic Inventory Reservation:** The instant an item is placed into an active cart, BandMath reserves that stock so two bandmates ringing up fans on separate phones can never accidentally double-sell the last Medium shirt.
* **Cash, Tap to Pay & Stripe QR Checkout:** Accept [Tap to Pay on iPhone & Android](../Accept_Payments/01_Tap_to_Pay.md), [Stripe QR Codes](../Accept_Payments/02_Stripe_QR_Codes.md) (ideal for international tours), or [Physical Cash](../Accept_Payments/03_Accepting_Cash.md) with explicit cash custody tracking.
* **At-Cost Member Purchases, Discounts & Tips:** Toggle between **Retail** and **At Cost** for band member purchases, apply custom cart discounts, or log fan tips on the fly.
* **Customer Email Receipts:** Send digital receipts straight from the checkout screen.

## 3. Event Merch Window & Closing Room Integration

Every sale rung up in the **Merch Stand** during a show's **Event Merch Window** (`3:00 PM – 4:00 AM`) is automatically linked to that night's show in the [Multi-Band Closing Room](../Events_and_Closing_Room/04_Multi_Band_Closing_Room.md)—ready for net-of-tax, net-of-Stripe venue merch cut calculations without any manual spreadsheet tallying.
