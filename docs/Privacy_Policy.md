# Privacy Policy

### 1\. The Short Version

We built BandMath to help musicians, bands, venue managers, and tour managers/promoters manage their finances, ticketing, and show settlements—not to mine your personal data. We only collect the information we absolutely need to make the platform function, process subscriptions, deliver fan tickets and receipts, and generate accurate show settlements. We will never sell your personal data to advertisers or third parties.

### 2\. What Information We Collect

To provide the BandMath service, we collect:

*   **Account & Organization Data:** Your email address, profile information, and organization role (Artist/Band, Venue, or Tour Manager/Promoter) to create and secure your account.
*   **Financial, Settlement & App Data:** The numerical data, expense logs, inventory counts, ticket tiers, and show settlement parameters you input into the platform.
*   **Fan Transactional Order & Ticket Data:** When fans purchase pre-sale tickets online or request a digital receipt at an in-person point-of-sale (POS) terminal, we collect the customer's email address (and name for ticket holders) strictly to deliver QR tickets, purchase receipts, ticket transfer links, and critical event notifications.
*   **Opt-In Fan Mailing List Data:** Only when a fan explicitly opts in at online ticket checkout or on the in-person POS receipt screen do we record their email address in the respective Event Organizer's marketing mailing list.
*   **Interested Party & Offline Slot Contact Data:** Business contact details (name, email address, and optional phone number) entered by an Event Organizer or Closing Officer to deliver transactional Event Closing Statements to participating bands, venues, or promoters.
*   **User Uploads:** Any photos or documents you choose to upload for receipt tracking or event promotion.
*   **Payment Information:** If you activate a Merchant Services Subscription or purchase a ticket or merchandise item, your payment details are collected directly by Stripe. BandMath does not store or have access to your full credit card number.

### 3\. Fan Transactional Emails vs. Opt-In Mailing Lists (GDPR Legal Bases & RLS Isolation)

When fans interact with BandMath ticketing or point-of-sale checkouts, BandMath acts as a **Data Processor** hosting records on behalf of the specific Event Organizer (the Band, Venue, or Tour Manager/Promoter acting as the **Data Controller**). We enforce a strict technical and legal separation between transactional records and marketing lists:  
  
**1) Transactional Ticket & Receipt Records (Contractual Necessity & Legal Obligation — GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) & (c)):** Customer email addresses attached to orders and tickets are stored strictly to fulfill your purchase (delivering scannable QR tickets, emailed POS receipts, ticket transfer links, door guestlist verification, and refund or cancellation notices) and to maintain required tax and accounting ledger records. Providing an email for a POS receipt or buying a ticket never automatically subscribes you to marketing emails.  
  
**2) Organizer Mailing Lists (Explicit Opt-In Consent — GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)):** BandMath maintains a separate mailing list table for each Organizer that is populated **only** if you actively check the marketing opt-in option during online ticket checkout or on the in-person Merch/Door POS screen. All fan records are stored on encrypted cloud database infrastructure protected by strict database Row-Level Security (RLS) policies, ensuring each Event Organizer can only access records belonging to their own events and customers.

### 4\. Camera & Device Data (App Store Compliance)

To provide core functionality, the BandMath mobile app requests access to your device's camera. This access is strictly used for two distinct purposes: 1) capturing photos of physical receipts for your expense tracking, and 2) scanning ticket QR codes using the app's "Bouncer Mode" feature. We do not use the camera for facial recognition, we do not continuously record video, and we do not process camera data for advertising. You can revoke camera permissions at any time via your device settings.

### 5\. Stripe Connect, Ticketing & In-Person Payments

If your organization (Band, Venue, or Tour Manager/Promoter) uses our Stripe Connect integration to process online ticket sales or in-person fan payments via Tap to Pay on iPhone or Tap to Pay on Android, you will be required to provide identity verification (KYC) and business details directly to Stripe. This highly sensitive data is handled entirely by Stripe’s secure infrastructure. BandMath does not host or store your government ID, bank account routing numbers, or your fans' credit card data.

### 6\. Cross-Organization Show Room Visibility & Interested Party Contacts

**Show Room Collaboration:** When multiple organizations (such as a Venue, a Tour Manager/Promoter, and performing Bands) collaborate on a shared event using a 6-character Show Code, shared event-level data—specifically the event roster, shared Show Pot ticket counts and gross ticketing revenue, declared shared show expenses, and slot-level settlement allocations—is visible to the authorized organizations connected to that event.  
  
**Strict Ledger Isolation:** Linking to a shared Show Room never exposes your organization's private ledger, unrelated events, private band-only expenses, merchandise sales, fan mailing lists, or individual band member financial standings to other show participants.  
  
**Interested Party Contact Processing:** When a Closing Officer enters business contact details (such as a contact email) for an offline/unclaimed band slot, venue, or promoter, BandMath processes that email address strictly as a Data Processor on behalf of the Event Organizer for the sole purpose of delivering the privacy-scoped transactional Event Closing Statement. We never use third-party roster contact emails for marketing or promotional outreach.

### 7\. Third-Party Services We Use (Data Processors)

We run a lean operation and rely on a few trusted third-party categories of service providers (Data Processors) to keep the app running securely. We do not sell your data to these third parties; they are bound by strict data processing agreements and confidentiality obligations to only process your data on our behalf. Your data interacts with:

*   **Cloud Hosting & Database Providers:** To securely store your user account, app data, RLS-isolated fan records, settlement records, and receipt uploads.
*   **App Framework Providers:** To build and host the mobile and web interfaces you interact with.
*   **Payment Processors:** To process your Merchant Services Subscription and facilitate online ticketing and Tap to Pay on iPhone / Tap to Pay on Android transactions.
*   **Communication Platforms:** To deliver transactional emails (such as ticket receipts, POS receipts, transfer links, and Event Closing Statements) and account updates.

### 8\. AI Product & Support Guides (Ask BandMath & Talk to BandMath)

When you use our interactive AI product and documentation assistants ("Ask BandMath" and "Talk to BandMath"), your questions and the assistant's responses are logged anonymously using a random ephemeral session token—without storing IP addresses, user accounts, or tracking cookies—under our legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f) & Art. 13, and EU AI Act Art. 50) solely to identify documentation gaps and improve answer accuracy. Automated server-side redaction scrubs accidental email addresses and phone numbers prior to storage, and unpinned conversation logs are automatically purged after a rolling 90-day retention period; please avoid entering sensitive personal data into chat prompts.

### 9\. Data Retention

We retain your personal and financial data only for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide you with our services. If you choose to delete your account, all associated personal data (including your private ledger and receipt uploads) will be permanently deleted from our active databases within 30 days. We may retain certain anonymized transaction or multi-party settlement records for a longer period only if strictly required to comply with our legal, tax, or accounting obligations.

### 10\. Your Data Rights (GDPR)

You own your data. Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and equivalent international laws, you have the right to:

*   Ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you (which account holders can do via the native export tool in the app).
*   Request that we correct any inaccurate information.
*   Withdraw your consent for marketing emails at any time (including unsubscribing or requesting removal from an Event Organizer's opt-in mailing list).
*   Request that we completely delete your account and personal data (or remove your contact email from an Organizer's mailing list or offline show slot) from our active systems by contacting the Event Organizer or emailing support@itsbandmath.com.

### 11\. Cookies

We keep things simple and respect your privacy. The BandMath website and web portals only use strictly necessary cookies required for the site to function, to keep you securely logged in, and to route you to our payment processor. We do not use non-essential, invasive marketing or tracking cookies.
