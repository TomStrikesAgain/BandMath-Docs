# Venues & Tour Managers Quickstart

BandMath isn't just for bands on stage—it is built for the **Independent Venues**, **Promoters**, and **Tour Managers** who run the box office, manage the door, and settle the night's numbers in the back office.

When you select the **Venue** or **Tour Manager** persona during onboarding (or switch personas inside **User Settings**), BandMath transforms into a focused, high-speed box office and settlement console.

---

## 1. The Focused 3-Icon Navigation Bar

Venues and Tour Managers don't need T-shirt size matrices or band rehearsal calendars cluttering their screen during doors. Your bottom navigation bar is streamlined into three dedicated workspaces:

1. **Events:** Create new shows (`[ + CREATE EVENT ]`), unlock any existing show via its **6-Character Show Access Code**, manage the multi-band lineup, and run **The Closing Room** settlement table.
2. **Bouncer:** Open the dedicated **QR Ticket Scanner & Guest List** with the **Bouncer Lock** so door staff can check in fans without accessing financial tabs.
3. **Cart:** Ring up walk-up fans in seconds using **`[ QUICK SELL DOOR TICKETS ]`** via **Tap to Pay** or **Cash**, with a live item-count badge.

---

## 2. Your Dedicated Merchant Account & Stripe Payouts

Even though you aren't a multi-member band, BandMath automatically provisions a dedicated **Merchant Account & Cashbox** behind the scenes for your Venue or Tour Manager profile.

In **User Settings**, the standard `My Bands` card is replaced by the **`MERCHANT & STRIPE PAYOUTS`** hub, where you can:
* **Connect to Stripe:** Link your business or personal bank account via Stripe Connect so you can claim online pre-sale ticketing for shows you promote and accept contactless **Tap to Pay** at the box office.
* **Set Your Base Currency:** Choose the currency symbol (`€`, `$`, `£`, etc.) used across your events and door POS.
* **Manage Billing (`account.itsbandmath.com`):** Launch the dedicated **Billing-Only Merchant Portal** to manage your `€20/mo` Pro subscription or claim one of the strictly limited `€100` **Bootstrapper's Club** lifetime passes.

:::note Free Show Creation & Settlement Participation
Any free Venue, Tour Manager, or Band account can create an event, join a show via Show Code, and participate in the Closing Room settlement math at zero cost. A Merchant subscription and connected Stripe account are only required when you want to process live card payments (Online Pre-Sale Ticketing or Tap to Pay at the door).
:::

---

## 3. Core Night-of-Show Workflow for Venues & Tour Managers

### Step 1: Create or Unlock the Show
* **Creating the Show:** Tap **`[ + CREATE EVENT ]`** on the **Events** tab to set the event title, date, venue details, ticket tiers, and the agreed **Event Ticket Tax Rate**. BandMath automatically generates a unique **6-Character Show Access Code**.
* **Unlocking a Band-Created Show:** If a band on the bill already created the event in BandMath, simply tap **Unlock / Join by Show Code** and enter their 6-character code to attach your Venue or Tour Manager account to the show.

### Step 2: Share the Show Code with the Bill
Give the 6-character Show Code to the Headliner, Co-Headliner, Support, and Opener acts. Each band taps **`[ JOIN SHOW ]`** in their own BandMath app—automatically linking their band's pre-sale referral link (`?ref=`), POS merch window, and settlement envelope to the shared Closing Room.

### Step 3: Sell & Scan Tickets at the Door
* **Door Sales:** Tap **`[ QUICK SELL DOOR TICKETS ]`** to ring up walk-up admissions in **Cash** or **Tap to Pay**. Because BandMath uses **Multi-Custodian Door POS**, any door money you collect lands in your own Merchant Account / Cashbox and is automatically tracked under `Door Tickets by Custodian` on the Closing Table.
* **Door Check-In:** Open the **Bouncer** tab, enable the **Bouncer Lock**, and hand the device to your door person to scan fan QR tickets and check off the Guest List.

### Step 4: Settle the Room & Email Final Receipts
At the end of the night, open the **Closing Room** inside the event:
* Enter **Shared Show Pot** expenses and incomes (such as sound engineer fees, security, catering, or promoter guarantees) and review each band's net payout envelope.
* Tap **`[ EMAIL FINAL CLOSING RECEIPTS ]`** to send itemized, privacy-scoped HTML closing statements to the house, the tour manager, and every band on the bill in a single tap.

For a complete breakdown of show mechanics, continue to [Events & The Closing Room](../Events_and_Closing_Room/01_Creating_and_Joining_Shows.md).
