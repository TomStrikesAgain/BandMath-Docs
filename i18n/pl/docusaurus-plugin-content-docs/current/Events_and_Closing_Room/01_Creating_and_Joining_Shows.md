# Creating & Joining Multi-Band Shows

Live shows are rarely solo operations. On any given night, a show brings together a **Venue**, a **Promoter / Tour Manager**, a **Headliner**, and one or more **Support / Opener** bands. BandMath connects every party on the bill into a single shared event using a **6-Character Show Access Code**.

---

## 1. Anyone Can Create the Show (100% Free)

You never need to wait on a specific person to set up the show in BandMath:
* **Bands:** Tap **`[ + CREATE EVENT ]`** inside the **Events** tab to create a show for your band or a multi-band bill.
* **Venues & Tour Managers:** Tap **`[ + CREATE EVENT ]`** inside your 3-tab settlement hub to create the event on behalf of the house or tour package.

Creating an event, adding ticket tiers, inviting bands to the lineup, and running the **Closing Room** math is **100% free** for every account type.

---

## 2. The 6-Character Show Access Code (`[ JOIN SHOW ]`)

Every event in BandMath automatically generates a unique **6-Character Show Access Code** visible in the event details header.

### Joining as a Band (`Headliner`, `Co-Headliner`, `Support`, `Opener`)
When your band arrives at the venue (or when advancing the show weeks beforehand):
1. Open the **Events** tab and tap **`[ JOIN SHOW ]`**.
2. Enter the 6-character Show Access Code shared by the Venue, Tour Manager, or originating Band.
3. Choose your billing role (`Headliner`, `Co-Headliner`, `Support`, or `Opener`) and optional stage time.

### Joining Mid-Show (Retroactive Merch & Split Sync)
Forgot to join the show code before doors opened? **No problem.** A band can join a show via **`[ JOIN SHOW ]`** at any time—even mid-show or right before walking into the settlement room:
* **Retroactive Merch Pickup:** BandMath automatically links any POS merchandise sales your band rang up during that day's **Event Merch Window** (`3:00 PM – 4:00 AM`) to your band's Show Call Sheet.
* **Dynamic Shared-Pot Rebalancing:** Any unsettled equal-split (`1/N`) shared show expenses or incomes automatically rebalance from `1/N` to `1/(N+1)` across the updated lineup without disturbing any items that were already marked settled.

### Unlocking as a Venue or Tour Manager
If a band created the event first, the Venue or Tour Manager can enter the exact same **6-Character Show Code** in their **Events** hub to unlock the show, ring up Door Tickets, scan fans in Bouncer Mode, and coordinate the Closing Room.

---

## 3. Run-of-Show Stage Times vs. The Event Merch Window

BandMath cleanly separates **when a band is on stage** from **when a band is selling merch**:

| Schedule Window | How It Works |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Run-of-Show Stage Slot** (`set_start_time` & `set_duration_minutes`) | Optional stage time (e.g., `8:15 PM · 45 mins`) displayed on the Show Call Sheet and linked to your [Setlist Organizer](../Features/05_Setlist_Organizer.md). |
| **Event Merch Window** (`3:00 PM – 4:00 AM`) | The shared full-night window during which every band on the bill sells merchandise at the venue. Your stage slot **never** restricts when your Merch Stand sales count toward the show! |
