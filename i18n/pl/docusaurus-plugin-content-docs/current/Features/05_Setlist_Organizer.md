# The Setlist Organizer

Great tours run on tight sets. Built directly into your band workspace alongside your shows and calendar, BandMath's **Setlist Organizer** lets your band build a reusable song catalog, craft show-specific setlists, and time your performance down to the minute.

---

## 1. Building Your Master Song Catalog

Instead of typing out song titles from scratch before every gig, you maintain a central **Song Catalog** for your band:
* **Song Details:** Store each song's title, duration (`mm:ss`), musical key, tempo (BPM), tuning notes, and transition cues (e.g., *Segues directly into Track 4* or *Capo 2*).
* **Shared Across the Band:** Every member of your band has live access to the updated catalog on their own phone.

---

## 2. Crafting & Reordering Show Setlists

Whether you are playing a 30-minute festival opener slot or a 90-minute headlining set, you can build custom setlists and link them directly to upcoming shows on your [Band Calendar](./08_Band_Calendar_and_Events.md):

1. **Add Songs from Your Catalog:** Tap songs from your master catalog to drop them into the setlist, or add one-off covers, medleys, and stage banter/tuning breaks.
2. **Drag-and-Drop Reordering:** Audition different set flows by dragging songs up or down the list.
3. **Encore & Set Dividers:** Group songs into main set and encore blocks so the pacing is crystal clear on stage.

---

## 3. Automatic Runtime vs. Stage Slot Timing

Curfew penalties at venues are real. As you add, remove, or reorder songs in a setlist, BandMath automatically calculates your **Total Set Duration**:
* **Live Runtime Tally:** See your cumulative music runtime updated instantaneously as you tweak the order.
* **Stage Slot Comparison:** When your band is assigned a **Run-of-Show Stage Time** (`set_start_time` and `set_duration_minutes`, such as a `45-minute` support slot) on an Event, you can immediately see whether your setlist leaves enough breathing room for tuning and stage transitions before your changeover.

:::tip Stage Times Never Restrict Your Merch Table
Setting a `45-minute` stage slot (`8:00 PM – 8:45 PM`) on a show helps everyone stay on schedule for the Run of Show and Setlist Organizer, while your band's **Event Merch Window** remains open across the full show day (`3:00 PM – 4:00 AM`) so every shirt sold before doors or after the headliner counts toward your show summary.
:::
