# Events Dashboard & Band Calendar

Tour routing, rehearsal schedules, studio lockouts, and multi-band show settlements all live inside the **Events** tab. BandMath lets you toggle effortlessly between the chronological **Events List** and the interactive **Band Calendar View** so your entire band always knows where they need to be and when.

---

## 1. Events List vs. Interactive Band Calendar

At the top of the **Events** tab, you can switch between two synchronized views:
* **Events List View:** A focused, chronological feed of upcoming and past events with quick access to ticket links, Bouncer QR scanning, setlists, and the **Closing Room**.
* **Band Calendar View:** An interactive monthly and agenda calendar that visually color-codes your band's schedule so you can spot routing gaps, double-bookings, and member blackouts at a glance.

---

## 2. Supported Calendar Event Types

Tour life is more than just show nights. When adding an entry to the **Band Calendar**, you can categorize it by type so everyone in the band knows what the day entails:

| Event Type | Best Used For |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Show / Concert** | Full live performances with ticketing, Run-of-Show stage times, setlists, and **Closing Room** settlement. |
| **Rehearsal** | Practice space sessions, pre-tour production rehearsals, and soundchecks. |
| **Studio Date** | Tracking, mixing, or mastering sessions with studio call times and notes. |
| **Travel Day** | Long van drives, flights, ferry crossings, and hotel check-in details. |
| **Promo / Press** | Radio sessions, podcast interviews, photo shoots, and content days. |
| **Blackout / Unavailable** | Dates when one or more band members have personal commitments or work conflicts so nobody books a gig by mistake. |

---

## 3. Owned Shows + Joined Multi-Band Shows in One Calendar

On tour, your band won't always be the one creating the event in BandMath—often the **Venue**, **Tour Manager**, or **Headliner** creates the show and hands you a **6-Character Show Access Code**.

Whenever your band taps **`[ JOIN SHOW ]`** and enters a Show Code, BandMath automatically merges that joined multi-band event directly into your **Events List** and **Band Calendar View** alongside the events your band created.

:::tip
Tap any **Show** on your Band Calendar to jump straight into its event details—where you can grab your band's [Promoter Attribution Link (`?ref=`)](../Events_and_Closing_Room/02_Online_Ticketing_and_Promoter_Links.md), check your Run-of-Show stage time, attach your [Setlist](./05_Setlist_Organizer.md), or open [The Closing Room](../Events_and_Closing_Room/04_Multi_Band_Closing_Room.md).
:::
